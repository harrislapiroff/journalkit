# Explanation

Why journalkit is built the way it is. Nothing here is needed to use it, but
each page explains a decision that shapes what you see on the page and what
`journalkit check` refuses to let slide.

- [The two grids](two-grids.md) — a module grid and a dot lattice, both
  anchored to the page, and what "commensurate" means.
- [Mirroring](mirroring.md) — why margins are `inner` and `outer`, and why a
  module never knows which side it is on.
- [Fonts and baselines](fonts.md) — how text is positioned, and why a missing
  font is the one failure that looks like success.
- [Dots and labels](dots-and-labels.md) — why dots are dropped rather than
  moved.
- [Design principles](principles.md) — the short list behind all of the above.
